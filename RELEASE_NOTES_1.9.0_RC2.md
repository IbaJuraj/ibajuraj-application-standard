# IbaJuraj Application Standard 1.9.0 RC2

**Candidate date:** 25 September 2026  
**Stable authority remains:** 1.8.0 (`standard-v1.8.0`)  
**Proposed candidate tag:** `standard-v1.9.0-rc2`

RC2 preserves the 121-rule RC1 candidate and adds 10 release-quality contracts for **131 machine-readable rules**.

## Central change
A complete whole-app audit is mandatory when a build is nominated for App Store/store/production release, **not after every small development build**.

Reference architecture: **IbaJuraj Release Inspector / Quality Engine** with In-App Full App Check, release diff/risk audit, critical user journeys and failure injection, visual/runtime matrix, governed AI review, exact-build Evidence Bundle and post-release feedback where supported.

AI is advisory and explainable; it is not the sole PASS/FAIL authority and it must use minimized/sanitized inputs.

Stable public authority remains 1.8.0 until pilot and cross-app RC2 evidence is complete.
